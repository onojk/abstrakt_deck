DESCRIPTION   [up to 10,000 chars]
-----------
Abstrakt Deck is a real-time visualizer that turns whatever your PC is
playing into shifting kaleidoscopes, 3D forms and painterly patterns.

It listens to system audio by default, so it reacts to music from any app
with nothing to configure. Prefer a live source? Switch to your microphone.
Want a finished video? Load an audio file and export a music video at up to
4K.

Under the hood it tracks tempo and beats across frequency bands, so the
visuals don't just pulse with volume - they lock to the rhythm.

Build your own look from shapes, painters and effects, save it as a preset,
or hand control to Random, Reactive or Party mode and let the music decide.
Drive it from a MIDI controller for live sets, go fullscreen with F11, and
press F12 to record what you see.

Everything runs locally on your PC. Abstrakt Deck does not collect, store
or send any data.

NOTES FOR CERTIFICATION   [Submission options > Notes for certification]
-----------------------
Abstrakt Deck is a Rust/wgpu desktop app packaged as a full-trust MSIX.

- runFullTrust: required for the desktop (Win32) executable.
- microphone: only used when the user selects Audio > Source > Mic. The
  default source is Loopback, which captures system audio output via
  WASAPI loopback and needs no permission prompt.
- To test: play music in any app, then launch Abstrakt Deck - the visuals
  react immediately. F11 toggles fullscreen, M hides the side panel,
  F12 starts/stops a recording (saved to Videos\abstrakt-deck).
- ffmpeg.exe (GPLv3) is bundled and run as a separate process only to
  encode recordings/exports. License texts are in the Licenses folder of the
  package; FFmpeg source: https://onojk123.com/abstrakt-deck/ffmpeg-7.1.1.tar.xz
- No network access, no account, no data collection.
- MIDI input is optional; with no MIDI device the app runs normally.
